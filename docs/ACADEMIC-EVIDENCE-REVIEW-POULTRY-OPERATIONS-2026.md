# SmartFarm Academic Evidence Review — Poultry Operations
Date: 2026-10-08

## Objective
Translate credible literature into measurable farm operations. SmartFarm remains an operational record system, not a veterinary diagnostic system.

## Operational evidence priorities
### 1. Thermal environment
Heat stress has measurable effects on feed intake, body-weight gain, FCR and mortality. SmartFarm should therefore support temperature/humidity observations, alarms/notes, water interruptions and heat-event logs.
Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.psj.2020.08.019

### 2. Climate adaptation
A 2026 African scoping review supports region-specific climate adaptation. SmartFarm should not hard-code a single “ideal” environmental profile; it should record local conditions and farm responses.
Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.psj.2026.107634

### 3. Biosecurity
Evidence supports treating cleaning, disinfection and biosecurity as auditable operational controls, while also recognizing that intervention-specific evidence remains incomplete.
Sources:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.psj.2024.103955
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.psj.2025.106232

### 4. Antimicrobial stewardship
Track medication/product, reason, authorizing professional where applicable, date, dose instructions as supplied by the responsible professional, withdrawal requirements, and outcome. Do not generate treatment instructions autonomously.
Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jgar.2024.09.010

### 5. Feed
Record feed type, batch, quantity, cost, supplier, dates and birds/cycle. Alternative feed ingredients require evidence for safety, processing and inclusion level before operational adoption.
Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.psj.2026.107730

## KPI definitions
At minimum:
FCR = feed consumed / live-weight gain, with numerator/denominator source and period recorded.
Mortality % = deaths / birds placed × 100.
Average final live weight = final live weight / birds marketed, subject to agreed sampling/recording method.
Cycle length = placement-to-marketing interval.

Never compare KPIs across farms unless definitions, periods, strains, production systems and measurement methods are compatible.

## Digital infrastructure
Starlink is connectivity infrastructure, not a substitute for offline operation. CCTV is a security system, not production evidence by itself. Keep access control, audit logs, retention and privacy boundaries explicit.
