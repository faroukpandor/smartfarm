# Integrated Poultry & Livestock Pilot

## Purpose

Define a reusable SmartFarm pilot for a commercial Botswana farm combining poultry, cattle/genetics and connected-farm infrastructure.

## Pilot enterprises

- Broiler production
- Stud Limousin cattle
- Future poultry processing, subject to verification
- Farm security/connectivity

## Core records

Poultry: house, flock, placement, feed, water, authorised health records, mortality, weights, costs, sales/collection and production KPIs.

Cattle: animal identity, pedigree/registration fields, breeding events, herd movements, weights, authorised health events and sales.

Infrastructure: poultry houses, equipment, CCTV assets, Starlink/network assets, power/backup and maintenance.

## Integration principle

SmartFarm = operational system of record.
AgriSage360 = intelligence, advisory and agricultural enterprise layer.
MOKORO = commercial-development and market-access layer.

Do not tightly couple applications merely to support one partner.

## KPIs

Birds placed; birds sold/collected; mortality; average weight; FCR where data exist; feed consumed; production cost; gross margin; house utilisation; cycle duration; sales/offtake pipeline; cattle indicators; infrastructure maintenance.

## Evidence and safety

No invented data. No automated veterinary diagnosis. No claim that CCTV or Starlink data are integrated until a permitted technical integration exists. Records must remain exportable.

## UI standard

Use the Collective cross between Napkin AI and Gamma AI: visual dashboards, one-decision-per-screen where possible, narrative farm reports, cards/timelines, simple charts, relationship diagrams, evidence/status indicators and mobile-first/offline-first operation.
