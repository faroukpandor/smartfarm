# Livestock Botswana / SmartFarm: Evidence-Led Content Programme

**Prepared:** 21 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Build a locally useful and commercially credible content cluster from Botswana and Southern African livestock research.  
**Publishing rule:** Publish only after source, date, rights, local applicability and claims have been checked.

## Positioning

**Editorial proposition:** Practical cattle enterprise economics for Botswana's real production environments.

The competitive advantage is not claiming to have invented a new grazing system. It is translating peer-reviewed research into clear decisions for commercial ranches, cattle posts, communal producers, emerging farmers and agricultural service providers.

Every major article should answer:
1. What problem does this solve for a Botswana producer?
2. What does peer-reviewed evidence actually show?
3. What is uncertain or context-specific?
4. What can a farmer measure without expensive equipment?
5. What decision might the evidence inform?
6. What requires a qualified veterinarian, range specialist, laboratory or official authority?

### Pillar F — Beef markets and value chains

- Understanding buyer specifications before finishing cattle.

- Tool: buyer specification and sale-lot record.

### Pillar G — Soil microbiomes and indigenous bio-intelligence
- What the 2024 Botswana soil microbiome study reveals.
- Sandveld, Wet-Sandveld and Hardveld: why microbial communities differ.
- What we still do not know about microbes and forage productivity.
- Research opportunities in drought-associated bacteria, fungi, mycorrhizae and nutrient cycling.
- No microbial inoculant efficacy or productivity claim without controlled trials and regulatory review.

## Content formats

For each flagship article, produce:
- One long-form evidence article.
- One 600–900-word farmer-facing summary.
- One short social post with one clear question.
- One 60–90-second video script.
- One printable checklist or worksheet.
- One source note with DOI links, publication date, evidence grade and claim limitations.

Do not turn every article into a sales pitch. Use a service call-to-action only where a real service can be delivered and the scope, price and qualifications are clear.

## Editorial quality gate

Before publication:
- Confirm DOI resolves and metadata matches publisher/PubMed/repository record.
- Confirm author list, title, journal, year, volume, issue and pages.
- Distinguish peer-reviewed evidence, government statistics, practitioner material and proposed hypotheses.
- Do not use an abstract as if it were a complete methods/results review.
- Date-stamp prices, policies, disease notices and buyer specifications.
- Obtain permission for interviews, photographs and farm case studies.
- Obtain qualified review for clinical, veterinary, rangeland prescription or regulatory claims.
- State when a claim is not yet validated in Botswana.
