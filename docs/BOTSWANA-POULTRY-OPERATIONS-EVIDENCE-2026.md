# Botswana Poultry Operations Evidence
Date: 2026-10-08

## Local operating benchmarks
A Botswana commercial-farm study found meaningful performance differences by region and house design, including EPEF differences and house-type effects on feed, gain, water and profitability.
Source: https://researchhub.buan.ac.bw/handle/13049/788

A Greater Gaborone farmer survey found common heat-mitigation approaches but concluded observed strategies were generally ineffective, reinforcing the need to measure actual farm outcomes rather than assume that a mitigation measure works.
Source: https://researchhub.buan.ac.bw/handle/13049/745

## SmartFarm implementation
For each house/cycle capture:
- house type/design
- location
- placement and harvest dates
- birds placed/marketed
- daily/weekly mortality
- feed quantity/cost
- water availability
- environmental observations
- cooling/ventilation interventions
- body-weight samples
- FCR
- EPEF/PEF where used
- veterinary/biosecurity events
- downtime
- revenue/direct costs

Every KPI must carry a source, date and calculation method.

## Alert philosophy
Do not invent universal thresholds from literature. Store literature reference ranges separately from farm-specific operating limits agreed with the responsible technical/veterinary team.
